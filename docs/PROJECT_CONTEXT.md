# Project Context

## Working Name

**Lemoore Student Success Assistant**

The name can be changed later without changing the product definition.

## Original Problem

Lemoore College has limited personnel and business-hour availability for student questions. Students may reach voicemail, wait for callbacks, miss details in proactive emails, or be transferred because student ambassadors cannot quickly locate the right information.

Many questions concern information that already exists online, but students do not know where to find it. Non-traditional and adult learners may need help outside normal office hours. Delayed or missing answers can create enrollment and persistence barriers.

The college also has varying course schedules, including eight-week courses and classes that start at different times. This means census and drop dates can differ and must not be answered using a generic date without checking the correct course or section.

## Users

### Prospective or Current Student

Needs fast, simple, trustworthy answers from official information.

### Student Ambassador

Needs a one-stop information tool to avoid transferring callers unnecessarily.

### College Staff/Admin

Needs visibility into repeated questions, unanswered topics, and documentation gaps.

## Product Promise

Provide immediate, understandable answers grounded in approved college sources, with citations and an honest path to human help when the answer cannot be verified.

## Success Metrics for the Demo

- The assistant answers representative questions with visible official citations.
- Unsupported questions safely escalate instead of hallucinating.
- A student can complete the flow on a phone.
- An ambassador can sign in and copy a response for a caller.
- Feedback is recorded.
- The dashboard shows useful aggregate information.
- The team can explain how AWS services support security, retrieval, scalability, and cost control.

## Key Differentiator

This is not merely a chat interface. It combines:

- official-source retrieval,
- citation transparency,
- role-aware assistance,
- deadline-aware information handling,
- safe escalation,
- and communication-gap analytics.

## Constraints

- Hackathon timeline
- Limited budget after the event
- No student-information-system integration in the MVP
- No private student records in the knowledge base
- Answers must be understandable to non-technical users
- Judges must understand the value quickly

## Lemoore College DSPS Mission

In alignment with the college mission, DSPS encompasses a diverse community of students with a variety of disabilities who are determined to reach their goals of obtaining a certificate and/or degree and/or transferring to a four-year institution. DSPS offers an engaging educational experience providing a full range of case management services, including advising and counseling, outreach, accommodations, and much more. These services cultivate an inclusive, balanced, and collegial climate for students with disabilities.

For more information about DSPS, visit the DSPS Office in Room 270B or [call the DSPS office during business hours](https://lemoorecollege.edu/resources/student-support/disabled-student-services/hours.php).

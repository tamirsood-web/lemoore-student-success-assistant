# Golden Nest: Food and Basic Needs Center

Lemoore College

We're here to make it easier for you to get the support you need—no judgment, just help. The Golden Nest connects you to food, housing, financial, and other resources on campus and in the community, all in one convenient spot at Lemoore College.

Drop by The Golden Nest and see what new resources are here for you! We are located in the Student Union Building 900, across from the Eagle Help Desk.

Student Basic Needs Assistance

##### Student Basic Needs Assistance

Are you a Lemoore College student facing challenges securing food, housing, or other basic needs? You can apply for additional financial assistance intended to support basic needs and your academic progress towards graduation.

Supplemental Grants are NOT available at this time.

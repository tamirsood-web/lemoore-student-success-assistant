We're here to make it easier for you to get the support you need—no judgment, just help. The Student Pantry is part of the [Golden Nest: Food and Basic Needs Center](https://lemoorecollege.edu/student-life/food-and-basic-needs/) which connects you to food, housing, financial, and other resources on campus and in the community, all in one convenient spot at Lemoore College.

Drop by the student pantry and see what new resources are here for you! We are located in the Student Union Building 900, across from the Eagle Help Desk.

- The Student Pantry is open to all currently enrolled Lemoore College students. We do not require students to provide proof of needing resources. When visiting the the Student Pantry, show your Lemoore College Student ID card at the front door and let us know how many people you are sharing the food with. We will provide you with a hand basket to fill your selected items and offer you extras that we have available for the day. Additional resources and support is available through the [Golden Nest: Food and Basic Needs Center](https://lemoorecollege.edu/student-life/food-and-basic-needs/) Please note, the Student Pantry does not currently provide services to employees of the college.

- October 2013, Lemoore College, Sociology Faculty Member, Dr. Vera Kennedy and students from the interdisciplinary studies Leadership class created the food security project by establishing the Eagle Pantry Club, to find ways to support our current Lemoore College student experiencing food insecurity challenges. The clubs constitution was approved on October 4, 2013. In the fall of 2014, the Eagle Pantry became a chapter of the Food Recovery Network. Food Recovery Network, Inc. (FRN or FRN National) unites students at colleges and universities to fight food waste and hunger by recovering surplus perishable food that would otherwise go to waste and donating it to people in need. May 2014, the club began food distributions on campus with donations from the local community including farmers, food distributors, residents, faculty, staff, and students. The Eagle Pantry Club initiated and has maintained a partnership with Panera Bread since 2017 that provides weekly distributions to students. In 2017, The Eagle Pantry Club entered into a partnership with the Community Food Bank mobile distributions services led by student volunteers to help unload, sort, and prepare food for community and campus distribution. November 2021, Kings Community Action Organization (KCAO) with CalViva Health committed to support and donate start-up equipment and a steady supply of food to supplement the current food pantry efforts from a two-shelf emergency pantry inside a storage closet to a grocery-like setting on-campus at Lemoore College to provide groceries at no cost to Lemoore College Students. December 2021, the Associated Student Government students requested the renovation of their designated space inside the Student Union to help make the grocery-like setting on campus. March 16, 2022, Lemoore College will host a ribbon-cutting ceremony for the on-campus **Golden Eagle Pantry** on March 16, 2022, from 12:00 pm – 1:00 pm in the campus Student Union (Building 900). August 11, 2025, Lemoore College opened **"The Golden Nest: Food & Basic Needs Center!"**, a student-voted name and tagline for the new **Basic Needs Center**. Designed to support student success, the center offers a grocery-style setting with access to food, community resources, and a dedicated space for community partners to connect directly with students. *The Student Pantry is a result of an entire community coming together. Through collaboration with amazing partners, the generosity of donors, and our staff and volunteers’ dedication, we give thanks to all who share in our commitment to end food insecurity.*

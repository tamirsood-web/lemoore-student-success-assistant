### Counseling/Advising Services

Welcome to Lemoore College’s counseling services! We serve a diverse student population with academic, career, transfer and personal counseling needs. Counseling faculty and staff are committed to providing students information to educate them on how to achieve their goals through educational plans and referrals to resources on campus and the community. Our goal is to show students they belong at Lemoore College, believe they will be successful and become who they plan to be.

### Counseling/Advising Services

### General / Academic Counseling

Need guidance with your educational journey? Our counselors provide support to help you succeed at Lemoore College. Services include:

- Educational planning and course selection
- Assistance with degree and certificate requirements
- Support for placement and registration
- Guidance for balancing school, work, and life
- Referrals to campus resources and support programs

### Probation / Academic Recovery

Struggling with academic progress? We’re here to help you get back on track. Through Academic Recovery services, you’ll find:

- Individualized counseling to create a tailored success plan
- Workshops on study skills and time management
- Academic probation advising and progress check-ins
- Access to tutoring and academic support resources
- Encouragement, accountability, and follow-up to meet your goals

### Career Counseling

Exploring career options or preparing for the workforce? Our Career Center and counseling team offer resources to guide your future. Services include:

- Career assessments to identify interests and strengths
- One-on-one career counseling and goal setting
- Resume and cover letter review and support
- Job search strategies, internships, and employer connections via the WIN Center
- Career readiness workshops and events

### Transfer Planning

Thinking of transferring to a fou-year university? Our team can help you plan your path toward a California State University, University of California or a private university. Here are a brief list of services offered:

- Workshops, planning tools, and application assistance
- ADT pathways with automatic placement
- GE certification options (CalGETC)
- Access to transfer reps from a range of four-year institutions
- Counseling and academic advising tailored to transfer goals

Visit our Transfer Services website for more details on upcoming activities and information.

# Returning Students

### Returning Student Enrollment Process

Returning Students are those students who last attended a class at Lemoore College or Coalinga College within the past 2 primary terms (Fall/Spring). If it has been more than 2 terms please visit our [Admissions Site](https://www.opencccapply.net/cccapply-welcome/?cccMisCode=582) to re-apply.

Continuing your educational journey at Lemoore College is easy! Simply follow the steps below to continue your journey!

[First Time Student](https://lemoorecollege.edu/admissions/) [Dreamers](https://lemoorecollege.edu/resources/dream-resource-center/) [High School Dual Enrollment](https://lemoorecollege.edu/admissions/high-school-dual-enrollment/) [Returning Student](https://lemoorecollege.edu/admissions/returning-students/)[Noncredit Education](https://lemoorecollege.edu/adult-education/index.php)

Congratulations! You've made it to third step of the admissions process. At Lemoore College, we require all new incoming students participate in an online orientation.

For your convenience, we offer students an online orientation available through our myWestHills student portal

To start your online orientation, please click on online student orientation below and log on using your myWestHills student account. If you do not have a myWestHills login, please complete an admission application first.

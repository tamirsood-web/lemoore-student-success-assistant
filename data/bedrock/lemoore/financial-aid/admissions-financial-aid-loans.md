## What Are Federal Direct Loans?

William D. Ford Direct Loans are low-interest loans for students to help pay for the cost of education. The lender is the U.S. Department of Education rather than a bank or other financial institution, though most of the contact will be with your loan servicer.

With Direct Loans, you:

- Borrow directly from the federal government and have a single contact—your loan servicer—for everything related to repayment, even if you receive Direct Loans at different schools.
- Have online access to your Direct Loan account information via your servicer's website.
- Can choose from several repayment plans, and you can switch repayment plans if your needs change.

> Please note that changes to the federal student aid loan program were signed into federal law via the [One Big Beautiful Bill](https://studentaid.gov/announcements-events/big-updates) and affect students who borrow student loans for the first time on or after July 1, 2026. Lemoore College is currently revising our Direct Loan processes to address these changes, so the Direct Loan application is not yet available. Check back for additional details soon.

## Types of Loans

### Subsidized Direct Loan - 6.52% Fixed Interest

While you are in school at least half-time, the federal government pays the interest on your subsidized loans. After you graduate, withdraw from school, or drop below half-time enrollment, there is a six-month grace period before repayment begins. When you enter repayment, you are responsible for repaying the amount borrowed and any interest that accrues during the repayment period.

### Unsubsidized Direct Loan - 6.52% Fixed Interest

Interest accrues immediately after disbursement. If you borrow unsubsidized loans, we strongly recommend that you begin making interest payments right after you receive your loan check. If you do not make interest payments while you are in school, the interest continues to accrue and is added (capitalized) to your principal loan balance at repayment. When interest capitalization occurs, you pay interest on top of interest, which increases both the amount of your loan and the total amount you repay. *This should be a loan of last resort.*

**Note:** Lemoore College does not participate in the Parent PLUS Loan or Private Alternative Loan Programs.

## Terms and Conditions

Loans must be repaid with interest. Students interested in borrowing Direct Loans must apply each school year.

To apply, you must:

1. Complete the [FAFSA](http://www.fafsa.gov/) and establish eligibility:
 - Meet Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) standards as determined by the Lemoore College Financial Aid Office.
 - Not be delinquent or in default on a federal student loan, or owe a refund on any federal or state grants.
 - Have remaining unmet need after grants, scholarships, and work-study programs have been considered.
2. Attend a Loan Workshop (call to schedule):
 - At the workshop, you will complete Entrance Counseling and a Master Promissory Note.
3. Complete the required Student Loan Request Form and Exit Counseling Form.
4. Be enrolled in and attending 6 or more degree-applicable units from your educational plan each semester.
5. Meet Lemoore College semester deadlines (typically the 10th week of the semester; contact the office to confirm current dates).

**Note:** Out-of-state students unable to appear in person for a Loan Workshop should contact Financial Aid immediately to coordinate correspondence via phone or email.

### Loan Processing Timelines

Financial Aid does not start processing Student Loan Requests until after the start of each Fall and Spring term. Loan funds are disbursed 4 to 6 weeks after submitting your request. These 4 to 6 weeks are in addition to financial aid processing timelines because eligibility for gift aid must be reviewed before loan consideration.

### Disbursements

Loan funds are disbursed in two equal amounts, minus origination fees. Full-year loan funds are disbursed in one standard disbursement per semester. Single-term loan funds cannot be disbursed in a single payment and must also be split into two disbursements. Disbursement cannot occur before at least 30 days into the semester and is generally made 6 to 8 weeks into the term.

Loan funds are first applied to direct institutional charges. Any remaining amount is then disbursed to the student.

### Exit Counseling

Loan Exit Counseling is required:

- If you drop below half-time enrollment (6.0 units).
- Within 30 days before graduating or leaving Lemoore College.

You must complete Loan Exit Counseling online at [StudentAid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/exit-counseling).

### Repayment

Direct Loans are a form of financial aid and must be repaid with interest. Generally, you have from 10 to 25 years to repay your loan, depending on the payment plan you choose. A variety of payment plans are available to meet your unique situation. Explore your repayment options at [StudentAid.gov/repay](http://www.studentaid.gov/repay), then contact your loan servicer to discuss a repayment plan that will work for you.

Interest subsidy during the six-month grace period was eliminated for loans made after July 1, 2012. The repayment period still begins six months after the student is no longer enrolled at least half-time, but the interest that accrues during those six months is payable by the student.

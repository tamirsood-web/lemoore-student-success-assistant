## Payment and Refund Request

Tuition and enrollment fees, payments, and refund requests for Lemoore College are handled by the Financial Aid / Business Office. Fee amounts and the full cost of attendance are listed on the official Cost of Attendance page.

**Requesting a refund:** Refund requests are submitted using the official online Refund Request Form linked from the Payment and Refund Request page. The Payment and Refund Policy and a Net Price Calculator are also linked from the official Cost of Attendance page.
